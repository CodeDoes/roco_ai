# Hearts and Academics: A Comprehensive Dating Sim TTRPG Adventure Guide

## Introduction and System Setup

Welcome, Game Master, to *Hearts and Academics*, a systemic, character-driven dating-simulator and slice-of-life tabletop roleplaying campaign guide designed for a party of 4 to 6 players. This comprehensive book serves as a multi-layered blueprint for your campaign, introducing relationship tracking grids, conversational subsystems, social combat rules, gift-giving matrices, and emotional crisis loops.

The setting of this adventure is the prestigious *Aethelgard Academy*, an elite boarding school situated on a beautiful, coastal estate in Western Europe. The characters are a group of transfer students entering their crucial senior year. Unlike a typical TTRPG where conflict is resolved with swords or plasma rifles, the challenges in this campaign are entirely social, academic, and emotional: players must maintain their GPA, balance their extracurricular schedules, navigate complex school rumors, and win the hearts of six unique and highly realized romance prospects.

As the GM, your role is that of a social director, gossip monger, and keeper of secrets. This book is written entirely in natural language to help you master these psychological and relational mechanics, the unique pacing of each school term, and the mechanical frameworks of each social act, allowing you to run a highly engaging, emotionally satisfying, and deeply memorable campaign where every choice is measured in affection, reputation, and personal growth.

## Act I: The Autumn Term - First Impressions

### Scene 1: The Opening Ceremony
The campaign begins on a crisp September morning. The players arrive at the historic, ivy-covered stone gates of Aethelgard Academy. The air is cool, smelling of fallen leaves, old books, and damp sea mist.

**Narrative Tone:**
The campus is a beautiful, expansive estate overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. Towering gothic archways, manicured lawns, and sweeping gravel paths are crowded with excited, well-dressed students in navy and gold blazers. The sounds of laughing, suitcases rolling on gravel, and the distant, rhythmic chime of the chapel bell echo through the courtyard, creating an atmosphere of fresh starts, high social stakes, and electric potential.

**The Romance Prospects (NPC Profiles):**
To run this game successfully, you must understand the six central romance options, their personalities, their academic profiles, and their secret vulnerabilities.

1. **Aria Sterling (The Class President):**
   - *Archetype:* The hyper-responsible, elegant perfectionist.
   - *Personality:* Formal, articulate, and highly disciplined. She carries the weight of her family’s high expectations on her shoulders and has no time for "childish distractions."
   - *Secret:* She is deeply insecure about her artistic passions, hiding a sketchbook of whimsical fantasy illustrations inside her formal leather planner.
   - *Likes:* Classic literature, herbal tea, quiet libraries, hand-written letters.
   - *Dislikes:* Tardiness, crude language, cheap gossip, chaotic environments.

2. **Julian Thorne (The Rogue Athlete):**
   - *Archetype:* The charming, rebellious captain of the rowing team.
   - *Personality:* Outgoing, athletic, and fiercely protective of his friends. He speaks with an easy, confident smile but struggles with his academic grades.
   - *Secret:* He is attending the academy on a fragile athletic scholarship, and if his grades drop below a C, he will be expelled. He is terrified of failing his family.
   - *Likes:* Sports trivia, physical training, competitive challenges, hot chocolate.
   - *Dislikes:* Strict rules, academic lectures, arrogant elites, being pitied.

3. **Evelyn Vance (The Reclusive Genius):**
   - *Archetype:* The quiet, sarcastic computer programmer and hacker.
   - *Personality:* Introverted, deeply analytical, and highly guarded. She spends her free time in the dark, basement computer lab, speaking in dry, ironic remarks.
   - *Secret:* She is the anonymous creator of *The Whispering Wall*, the academy's most popular, high-traffic gossip website, which she uses to monitor the school's social dynamics.
   - *Likes:* Coding, electronic music, cybernetic puzzles, dark-roast coffee.
   - *Dislikes:* Crowded social events, superficial flattery, physical contact, bright lights.

4. **Kaelen the Artist (The Melancholy Poet):**
   - *Archetype:* The dreamy, emotional fine-arts student.
   - *Personality:* Gentle, observant, and deeply expressive. He views the world through a lens of color and shadow, often speaking in poetic metaphors.
   - *Secret:* He is color-blind, a secret he guards fiercely to protect his scholarship in the fine-arts division, relying on carefully labeled paint tubes and his spatial memory.
   - *Likes:* Canvas paintings, autumn rain, vinyl records, deep philosophical chats.
   - *Dislikes:* Loud noises, physical sports, rigid schedules, lack of empathy.

5. **Beatrice the Heiress (The Social Butterfly):**
   - *Archetype:* The wealthy, trend-setting fashion icon.
   - *Personality:* Vivacious, charismatic, and highly manipulative. She commands the school's social circles with an iron hand wrapped in a velvet glove.
   - *Secret:* She is incredibly lonely, feeling that everyone is only friends with her because of her family's wealth and influence. She craves genuine, unselfish affection.
   - *Likes:* High-end fashion, social galas, expensive perfume, strawberry pastries.
   - *Dislikes:* Boring routines, old-fashioned rules, rainstorms, being ignored.

6. **Barnd the Underdog (The Hardworking Scholarship Student):**
   - *Archetype:* The earnest, clumsy student who works at the school cafe.
   - *Personality:* Optimistic, incredibly helpful, and resilient. He balances a full academic load with two part-time jobs, always smiling despite his exhaustion.
   - *Secret:* He was recently accused of cheating on an exam—a crime actually committed by a wealthy student who framed him to protect their own reputation.
   - *Likes:* Baking fresh pastries, warm fires, soft blankets, helping others.
   - *Dislikes:* Unfair accusations, social snobs, wasting food, extreme cold.

## Act II: The Conversational Combat and Relationship Mechanics

In *Hearts and Academics*, social interactions are structured using a unique "Conversational Combat" loop, where players use their intellectual and emotional skills to navigate discussions.

### The Affection Point (AP) and Social Standing Subsystem
Every romance prospect has an *Affection Tracker* from 0 to 100 AP, and a *Social Standing* metric representing their reputation within the academy's hierarchy.

**Conversational Combat Mechanics:**
During a social encounter (such as a lunch date or study session), the GM sets the "Social DC" and the "Emotional Vulnerability" of the prospect.
- *Active Listening:* A character can attempt a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check to identify the prospect’s current emotional state and passive tells.
- *Topic Matching:* The character must choose a conversational topic. Matching a prospect's Likes grants a +2 bonus to their next Charisma check. Choosing a Dislike imposes disadvantage.
- *The Roll:* The character makes a Charisma (Persuasion/Performance/Deception) check against the Social DC.
  - *Success (Met DC):* Prospect’s AP increases by +5. If they beat the DC by 5 or more, they trigger a "Special Dialogue" where the prospect reveals a personal secret or vulnerability.
  - *Failure (Missed DC):* AP remains unchanged, or decreases by -2 if the character was insensitive or crude.

## Act IV: The Winter Term - Rumors and Scandals

### Scene 1: The Whispering Wall
As the winter term begins, a major scandal erupts on the academy's gossip site, *The Whispering Wall*. A post anonymously accuses Barnd of stealing the answer key to the upcoming midterm calculus exam. The school administration is preparing to expel him.

**The Investigation Phase:**
The players must work together to clear Barnd’s name before the term ends in three weeks.
- *The Hacking Route:* The players can attempt to trace the IP address of the post. This requires a DC 14 Intelligence (Technology/Computers) check inside the computer lab, pointing toward Evelyn's computer console.
- *The Social Route:* The players can interview the student body. A DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion/Deception) check reveals that the wealthy student who actually stole the key is Beatrice’s cousin, who used Barnd's keys to access the faculty office.
- *The Confrontation:* The players must choose whether to confront Beatrice about her cousin's guilt.
  - *If Confronted Gently:* Beatrice breaks down, admitting she knew about her cousin's actions but was too terrified of her family's public disgrace to speak out. If the players support her (gaining +10 AP with Beatrice), she will personally deliver the confession to the Dean, clearing Barnd's name (+20 AP with Barnd).
  - *If Confronted Aggressively:* Beatrice retreats into her cold heiress persona, denying everything and permanently dropping her AP with the players by -20. Barnd is suspended, and the school’s morale drops.

## Act V: The Spring Gala - The Final Choice

### Scene 1: The Dance of Hearts
The campaign culminates at the beautiful, candle-lit Aethelgard Spring Gala, held in the grand ballroom overlooking the ocean. The air is warm, smelling of roses, sea breeze, and perfume.

**The Setup:**
To secure a romance prospect's heart and achieve the "True Ending," the player must have met the following conditions before the night of the Gala:
1. An Affection Level of at least 80 AP with their chosen prospect.
2. Completed that prospect's unique "Personal Resolution Quest" (such as helping Julian pass his exams, or discovering Kaelen's color-blindness and helping him label his paints).

**The Final Encounter (The Confession Scene):**
During the final dance, under a canopy of glowing fairy lights, the player can invite their chosen prospect to the balcony overlooking the sea. This is a purely roleplay-focused scene, where the player must express their genuine feelings, drawing upon the shared experiences, secrets, and vulnerabilities they discovered throughout the year.

*The True Endings:*
- **Aria’s Ending:** Aria agrees to let go of her perfectionist mask, choosing to pursue her passion for illustration alongside the player, her hand resting warmly in theirs as they look out at the sea.
- **Julian’s Ending:** Julian passes his exams and secures his scholarship. He smiles his classic, easy smile, but with a new, deep sincerity, promising to row his heart out for the player's future.
- **Evelyn’s Ending:** Evelyn shuts down *The Whispering Wall*, choosing to step out of the shadows and share her true, unedited self with the player, her cold sarcasm melting into a soft, genuine laugh.
- **Kaelen’s Ending:** Kaelen presents the player with a beautiful oil painting—painted entirely in black and white, but capturing the exact emotional light of their shared moments, a masterpiece of true feeling.
- **Beatrice’s Ending:** Beatrice lets go of her wealthy armor, crying tears of relief as she realizes someone finally loves her for who she is, not what she owns, her head resting securely on the player’s shoulder.
- **Barnd’s Ending:** Barnd’s name is cleared, and he is awarded a full scholarship. He bakes a special, warm pastry for the player, his hands shaking slightly with happiness as he promises to always be their anchor.

## Act VI: Advanced Social Mechanics, Romantic Tensions, and Gossip Networks

To truly capture the delicate complexity of a dating-simulator in a TTRPG format, a Game Master must employ systemic rules that govern rumors, rivalry, and the psychological state of romantic target options.

### The Gossip and Rumor Subsystem: "The Whispering Wall"

Rumors are a powerful, invisible currency at Aethelgard Academy. A single post on *The Whispering Wall* can instantly alter a character's social standing or damage their relationship with a romance prospect.

**Rumor Generation and Spread:**
At the start of each school week, the GM should roll 1d6 on the *Weekly Rumor Table* to determine what gossip is currently trending across campus.

#### Weekly Rumor Table (Roll 1d6):
1. **The Secret Rendezvous:** A post claims a player was seen meeting secretly with a rival prospect behind the boat house.
   - *Impact:* The player's primary romance prospect has their AP reduced by -10 due to jealousy, unless the player makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check to clear the misunderstanding.
2. **The Exam Leak:** Rumors circulate that a student in the Debate Club paid a teacher for exam questions.
   - *Impact:* Aria Sterling's stress level increases by +20%, making her Social DCs 2 points higher for the week as she launches a frantic investigation to protect the club's honor.
3. **The Financial Ruin:** A rumor spreads that Beatrice's family corporation is facing a massive tax fraud scandal in London.
   - *Impact:* Beatrice becomes highly defensive and irritable. Her AP decreases by -5 for every player who brings up the topic of family or wealth during dates.
4. **The Sports Doping Scandal:** An anonymous tip suggests the rowing team captain (Julian) has been using illegal performance-enhancing stimulants.
   - *Impact:* Julian’s confidence is shattered. He withdraws from social circles, requiring the player to personally find him at his training dock (DC 13 Perception) to offer emotional support (+15 AP if successful).
5. **The Plagiarized Painting:** A post accuses Kaelen of plagiarizing his latest landscape masterpiece from an obscure 19th-century French artist.
   - *Impact:* Kaelen locks himself in his art studio, refusing to speak to anyone. The player must slide a supportive letter under his door (DC 12 Sleight of Hand / Performance) to encourage him to open up.
6. **The Perfect Student:** A rare, positive post praises Barnd's tireless work ethic at the school cafe, encouraging students to tip him generously.
   - *Impact:* Barnd's stress level drops by 20%, and he offers free strawberry pastries to all players (+10 AP with Barnd if they visit him).

## Act VII: The Romantic Rivalry and Jealousy Subsystem

In a high-school boarding environment, players are not operating in a vacuum. Romance prospects have their own relationships, friendships, and potential rivalries with one another.

### Rivalry Mechanics and Love Triangles

If a player attempts to romance multiple prospects simultaneously (e.g., maintaining an AP level above 50 with both Aria and Beatrice), they trigger the *Romantic Tension/Rivalry Protocol*.

#### The Clashing Personalities:
1. **Aria vs. Beatrice (The Elite Clash):**
   - Aria views Beatrice as a superficial, spoiled socialite who uses her wealth to bypass rules. Beatrice views Aria as a rigid, boring bureaucrat who has never experienced true freedom.
   - *The Trap:* If a player takes Beatrice on a date to a high-end fashion boutique, and later that day attempts to study with Aria in the library, Aria will smell Beatrice's signature perfume on the player's blazer, immediately reducing her AP by -15.
2. **Julian vs. Kaelen (The Jock and the Poet):**
   - Julian is loud, physical, and competitive. Kaelen is quiet, introspective, and gentle. They struggle to understand each other's worldviews.
   - *The Trap:* If the player attends Julian's rowing regatta but leaves early to attend Kaelen's gallery exhibition, both prospects will notice. Julian’s confidence drops, and Kaelen’s melancholy deepens, reducing AP for both by -10.

#### Resolving the Tension:
To navigate a love triangle without permanently ruining their relationships, the player must host a joint social event (such as a group study session or a double date) and succeed on a difficult DC 15 Charisma (Insight and Persuasion) check to find common ground between the two rivals.
- **Success:** The rivals develop a mutual respect, resolving the tension and allowing the player to continue both relationships (though they must eventually choose one for the Spring Gala confession).
- **Failure:** Both prospects realize the player has been playing with their feelings. Both AP levels drop to 0, and they refuse to speak to the player for the rest of the term.

## Act IX: Running the Spring Gala - Step-by-Step DM Guide

The final act of *Hearts and Academics* is a high-stakes emotional climax. Here is a guide on how to structure the final scene to ensure maximum dramatic impact.

### Step 1: Setting the Ballroom Atmosphere
- Describe the sensory details: the soft, golden light of hundreds of candles, the scent of fresh white roses and lavender, the smooth velvet drapes, and the elegant, slow-tempo waltz played by a live string quartet.
- The ocean is visible through the massive glass archways of the ballroom, its waves catching the silver light of the full moon.
- Have all six prospects dressed in their finest formal wear, scattered across the room in their respective social circles.

### Step 2: The Final Term Summary
- Before the dancing begins, the Dean of Aethelgard Academy delivers a speech, summarizing the accomplishments of the senior class. This is an excellent opportunity for you, the GM, to publicly recap the players' achievements throughout the campaign (e.g., "Thanks to the efforts of our transfer students, Barnd’s name has been cleared, and Julian has secured his athletic future!").

### Step 3: The Dance Cards
- Each player receives a formal "Dance Card" with three slots. They can invite three different prospects to dance during the evening.
- Each dance is structured as a mini-social encounter (DC 13 Charisma (Performance or Persuasion)).
- If a dance is successful, it increases the prospect's AP by +5 and unlocks their final balcony invitation.

### Step 4: The Balcony Confession
- When the final waltz begins, the player must choose one prospect (who has at least 80 AP and whose personal quest is complete) and invite them to the private balcony overlooking the ocean.
- This is a safe space for pure roleplay. Encourage the player to deliver a sincere, personal confession, drawing upon the specific memories, inside jokes, and secrets they shared throughout the school year.
- *The Roll of Sincerity:* Do not have the player roll a standard Charisma check for the confession. Instead, evaluate the sincerity of their roleplay. If they reference the prospect's core vulnerabilities and personal growth, they automatically succeed, unlocking the "True Love Ending" and securing a beautiful, emotionally rich finale to your campaign!

## Act X: Extracurricular Activities, Special Locations, and Deep School Lore

To expand this slice-of-life dating simulator into a fully realized sandbox campaign, the Game Master should have access to a rich physical map, deep historical lore, and modular school club events.

### The Geography of Aethelgard Academy and Surrounding Areas

Aethelgard Academy is situated on a rugged peninsula known as the *Sentinel Cliffs*, overlooking the North Sea. The estate is completely self-contained, but senior students are allowed to visit the neighboring town of *Oakhaven-on-Sea* during weekend free-time.

1. **The Grand Library (The Sanctum of Whispers):**
   A towering, five-story gothic vault lined with hand-carved mahogany bookshelves and lit by stained-glass skylights. The library is famous for its "Whispering Chamber"—an acoustic anomaly under the central dome where even a tiny whisper can be heard clearly on the opposite side of the room.
   - *Academic Study Loop:* Spending an afternoon studying in the library grants a +2 bonus to any upcoming Intelligence or Wisdom checks for school exams. It is also the primary location to find Aria Sterling during free-time.

2. **The Boathouse and Training Docks:**
   Located at the base of the Sentinel Cliffs, accessible via a steep stone staircase. The boathouse smells of cedarwood, varnish, and salt water. This is where the rowing team conducts their grueling daily training sessions.
   - *Physical Training Loop:* Characters can participate in rowing practice (requiring a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check). Success grants a +2 bonus to Constitution checks for the next week and earns Julian Thorne’s admiration (+5 AP with Julian).

3. **Oakhaven-on-Sea (Weekend Town):**
   A picturesque coastal village of cobbled streets, colorful timber houses, and small antique shops. The town’s primary attraction is the *Anchor & Rose Cafe*, where Barnd works part-time.
   - *The Coffee Date:* Inviting a prospect to the Anchor & Rose Cafe costs 10 Credits and grants a flat +5 AP bonus. If the player purchases a *Strawberry Pastry* from Barnd while on the date, it triggers a humorous jealous dialogue from the prospect, adding rich comedic flavor to the social encounter.

Use these detailed locations, relationship mechanics, and step-by-step GM guides to run an unforgettable, emotionally satisfying dating-simulator TTRPG campaign!

## Act XI: Advanced Character Dialogue Paths and Interaction Branches

To support the Game Master in running interactive dialogues, this section provides explicit conversation trees and response options for critical romance milestones.

### Conversation Branch A: Aria Sterling's Sketchbook Discovery
*Triggered when a player finds Aria's hidden sketchbook in the library.*

- **Player Choice 1: "This is beautiful, Aria. Why do you hide such incredible talent?"**
  - *Aria's Response:* Her cheeks turn crimson, and she frantically tries to close the book. "Please... give that back. It’s... it’s nothing. Just a childish waste of time. My father says art is a distraction from real leadership."
  - *Follow-up Choice 1A: "Your father is wrong. True leadership requires vision, and this sketchbook is full of it."*
    - *Outcome:* Aria stares at you, speechless, her eyes glistening. **(+15 AP, unlocks Personal Quest: 'The Art Exhibition')**
  - *Follow-up Choice 1B: "Maybe he's right, but everyone needs a hobby. I won't tell anyone."*
    - *Outcome:* She relaxes slightly, nodding. "Thank you. I appreciate your discretion." **(+5 AP)**

- **Player Choice 2: "Class President draws fairy-tale dragons? I never would have guessed!"**
  - *Aria's Response:* She snatches the book away, her expression freezing into cold anger. "I would appreciate it if you didn't pry into my personal belongings. Now, if you'll excuse me, I have a council meeting to attend."
  - *Outcome:* Aria refuses to speak to the player for three days. **(-10 AP)**

Use these advanced dialogue branches to create truly interactive, emotionally resonant conversations that reward players for empathy and active listening!
